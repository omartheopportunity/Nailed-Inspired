Halloween Costumes for Black Women That Steal the Room

Sept 30, 2026 · 3 min read

These are the halloween costumes Black women are wearing this year: glam ones, funny ones, and fierce ones. Pick the category that matches your mood, then pick the look that feels like you.

Glam

  • Egyptian queen. Gold collar, cat-eye liner, and a structured headpiece. Add gold nails and the look is done.
  • Old Hollywood star. Satin gown, finger waves or a sleek bob, and bold red lips.
  • Disco diva. Sequins, platform boots, and a big statement afro or curls.
  • Fairy godmother. A glittery gown, a wand, and wings in your favorite color.

Funny

  • A stick of butter and a slice of bread. A duo costume that gets laughs at every party.
  • Human emoji. A yellow tee with a giant emoji face, and it costs almost nothing.
  • Your own group chat. Print speech bubbles and stick them on a black outfit.

Fierce

  • Warrior queen. Leather, armor-style accessories, and face paint in bold lines.
  • Vampire bride. A black gown, a deep berry lip, and lace gloves.
  • Cyber goddess. Silver bodysuit, chrome nails, and glossy braids or a slicked bun.

Styling tips

  • Match the makeup to your undertone. Rich golds, berries, and plums look great on deep skin. Choose pigment-heavy formulas that show up under party lighting.
  • Plan the hair first. Braids, curls, a wig, a silk-wrapped updo: pick the hair, then build the costume around it.
  • Do your nails to match. Chrome, glitter, or a deep burgundy French take the costume from good to complete.
Costumes are for one night. The version of you that walked in is the one who stays.
