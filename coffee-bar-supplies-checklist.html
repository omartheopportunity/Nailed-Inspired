Coffee Bar Checklist

Nov 17, 2026 · 2 min read

A good coffee bar is eight simple things. Save this checklist and build yours one item at a time.

1. Coffee Maker

Your daily workhorse.

2. Grinder

Fresh grounds.

3. Milk Frother

Cafe foam at home.

4. Mugs

A few you love.

5. Syrups

Two or three flavors.

6. Storage Jars

Beans and sugar.

7. Tray

Keeps it neat.

8. Cleaning Cloth

Wipe as you go.
