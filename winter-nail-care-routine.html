Winter Nail Care Routine

Nov 16, 2026 · 2 min read

Cold air dries out nails and cuticles. This six step routine keeps them strong, and it takes about five minutes.

1. Soak

Warm water for three minutes.

2. Push Back

Gently with a wooden stick.

3. Oil

Cuticle oil on every nail.

4. Cream

A thick hand cream at night.

5. Gloves

Wear them outside.

6. Top Coat

Refresh weekly to prevent chips.
