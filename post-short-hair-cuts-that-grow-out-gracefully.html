Short Hair Cuts That Grow Out Gracefully

Oct 1, 2026 · 3 min read

Not every short cut stays good for the same amount of time. If you do not want a salon visit every month, these short hair cuts are the ones that grow out well.

Best low-maintenance cuts

  1. Layered bob. Layers hide uneven growth.
  2. Shag. It is meant to look lived in.
  3. Long pixie. Extra length on top grows into a bob stage easily.
  4. Bixie. Soft layers grow out evenly.

Cuts that need more upkeep

  • Sharp blunt bobs lose their line quickly.
  • Short undercuts need clippers every few weeks.

The growth timeline

StageWhat it looks likeWhat to do
Month 1Fresh, definedStyle as the stylist showed you
Month 3Softer edgesAdd texture or a clip
Month 6Longer, a bit shaggyBook a shape-up

Tips to extend the time between trims

  • Ask for graduated layers instead of hard lines.
  • Use a texturizing spray to blend the grow-out.
  • Trim the neckline only and leave the length.
The best short cut is the one that still looks like you on day ninety.
