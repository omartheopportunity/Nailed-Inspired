Iconic Halloween Costumes for Women: Movie, Music, and Pop Culture

Sept 30, 2026 · 3 min read

These iconic halloween costumes women love to recreate come from film, music, and pop culture. Save the one you’d own, then use the pieces to build it.

Movie

  • Sandy in the black jumpsuit from Grease. Leather jacket, black satin pants, red heels, and curled hair.
  • Holly Golightly from Breakfast at Tiffany’s. Black gown, pearls, long gloves, and an updo with a tiara.
  • Dorothy from The Wizard of Oz. Blue gingham dress, ruby slippers, and braided pigtails.

Music

  • A 1980s pop icon. Neon, lace gloves, layered bracelets, and big teased hair.
  • A 1990s R&B group. Matching outfits, slick hair, and bold lip liner.
  • A rock star. Leather, fishnets, smoky eyes, and a guitar prop.

Pop culture

  • Rosie the Riveter. Blue work shirt, polka-dot bandana, and a rolled sleeve.
  • Frida Kahlo. Flower crown, braids, bold brows, and a colorful shawl.
  • Cleopatra. Gold collar, black bob wig, and dramatic eyeliner.

How to pull one off

  1. Choose the most recognizable element, like a hairstyle, hat, or one signature piece.
  2. Build around it with basics from your closet.
  3. Study a photo and match the makeup.

Respect the reference

If you’re dressing as a real person or a cultural icon, focus on the look and not a caricature. Pay tribute, not parody.
