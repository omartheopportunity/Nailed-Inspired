# Nailed & Inspired — Website Information Architecture
**Beauty is the beginning. Becoming is the mission.**

---

## 0. First, the flaw in the current 3-board structure

Your instinct to preserve 3 top-level doors is right — but two of the current three overlap:

- **"Ambitious Life"** and **"The Ambitious Woman"** both signal "ambition/lifestyle" with no clear line between them. A post about starting a side hustle could live in either. That ambiguity is exactly what breaks a taxonomy once you hit 100+ posts — you'll start guessing instead of knowing where things go.
- **"Confidence, Self-Worth & Feminine Energy"** is fine as-is — it's the one pillar that's actually distinct.

**Fix:** collapse the two overlapping pillars into one outward-facing "outcomes" pillar, and give the nail/style content — which is currently homeless, since none of the 3 boards actually mention nails — its own doorway. That also matches your real funnel: *Nails → Self-Image → Confidence → Identity → Community → Success* compresses cleanly into **3 doors that map to 3 funnel stages**, not 3 flat topics.

## 1. The Three Pillars (revised)

| # | Pillar | Funnel stage it owns | One-line promise |
|---|--------|----------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Self & Style** | Nails → Self-Image | "Start with how you show up." |
| 2 | **Confidence & Identity** | Confidence → Identity | "Become who you're actually becoming." |
| 3 | **Ambitious Life** | Community → Success | "Build the life and the room to match." |

This preserves nail content as the literal front door, keeps confidence/identity as the emotional core, and merges the two ambition boards into one pillar that's also your natural bridge into Lady's Leadershift / R3UP community.

## 2. Category Pages (the 3 pillars, in full)

### Pillar 1 — Self & Style
- **Description:** Nail inspiration, personal style, and the small daily choices that shape how a woman sees herself.
- **Who it's for:** Women who came in through a nail/style Pin and haven't yet framed this as "personal growth" — she just wants to look and feel put-together.
- **Subcategories:** Nail Inspiration & Trends · Personal Style & Everyday Aesthetic · Self-Image & How You Show Up · Beauty Rituals as Self-Care · Seasonal Refresh & Reinvention
- **Article types:** listicles ("30 fall things..."), nail/style guides, "what your X says about you," seasonal refresh checklists
- **Featured content:** your single best "gateway" post — the one most likely to convert a Pinterest click into a second pageview
- **Related categories:** Confidence & Identity (self-image → self-worth is the natural next click)
- **CTA:** soft — "Explore what's underneath the look" → link into Confidence & Identity, not a hard sell

### Pillar 2 — Confidence & Identity
- **Description:** The inner work — self-worth, mindset, feminine energy, and becoming intentional about who you're turning into.
- **Who it's for:** Women who've outgrown the surface-level content and are asking "who am I becoming" questions.
- **Subcategories:** Self-Worth & Inner Confidence · Feminine Energy (soft + strong) · Identity & Becoming · Mindset & Limiting Beliefs · Boundaries & Self-Respect
- **Article types:** identity-shift essays, mindset reframes, "signs you're becoming her," boundary-setting guides
- **Featured content:** your strongest identity/becoming essay — this is the emotional core of the brand, feature it prominently
- **Related categories:** both Self & Style (where she came from) and Ambitious Life (where she's headed)
- **CTA:** medium — email capture ("get the identity worksheet") + a nudge toward Ambitious Life

### Pillar 3 — Ambitious Life
- **Description:** Career, money, community, and the environment that makes an ambitious life sustainable — this is where the brand hands her off to the room.
- **Who it's for:** Women ready to act — leaving a 9–5, starting something, or looking for the right people around them.
- **Subcategories:** Career & Leaving the 9-5 · Entrepreneurship & Money · Discipline & Productivity · Community & Environment · Success Stories & Next Chapter
- **Article types:** career-transition guides, money mindset posts, "your environment matters" essays, community spotlights
- **Featured content:** a success story or environment-focused piece that leads directly into Lady's Leadershift
- **Related categories:** Confidence & Identity (the "why" behind the ambition)
- **CTA:** strong — direct link to Lady's Leadershift / "Start Your Next Chapter"

## 4. Article Template

Every post generated from a Pin follows this structure:

1. **SEO title** (search-friendly)
2. **Pinterest title** (curiosity/benefit-driven, can differ from SEO title)
3. **Short intro** (2–3 sentences, names the reader's felt experience)
4. **Main content** (the actual value/answer)
5. **Practical/actionable section** ("Try this," a checklist, or a short exercise)
6. **Related articles** (2–3 links, same subcategory)
7. **"You might also like"** (1 cross-pillar link — the bridge to the next stage of the journey)
8. **Category + subcategory tag line** (visible breadcrumb: Ambitious Life → Career & Leaving the 9-5)
9. **Email capture** (mid or end of article — see §11)
10. **CTA** (pillar-appropriate — see §12)

**Linking rule:** every article must link to at least 2 other articles in its own subcategory (depth) and exactly 1 article in an adjacent pillar (progression). Never link "backward" more than one stage in the funnel — don't send a Confidence & Identity reader back to a nail post as the featured next step.

## 5. Tagging System

- **Primary Category** = one of the 3 pillars (required, exactly one)
- **Subcategory** = one of the 5 under that pillar (required, exactly one)
- **Tags** = 3–6 controlled, reusable descriptors (e.g., `nails`, `personal style`, `confidence`, `self-expression`, `identity`, `career change`, `money mindset`, `community`)

**Rules for tags:**
- Maintain one master tag list. Before creating a new tag, check if an existing one already covers it — reuse beats proliferation.
- Only create a new tag when at least 3–5 existing/planned articles will use it (a tag with 1 use isn't a tag, it's a typo).
- Tags describe the *topic*, never duplicate the category/subcategory name.
- Review the tag list quarterly; merge or retire any tag under 3 uses.
- Target: fewer than 40 tags total even at 500 articles.

## 6. Pin → Blog Workflow (repeatable system)

Give this input each time: **Pin title + Pin description + article draft.**

Output produced every time:
1. **Primary pillar** (one of the 3)
2. **Subcategory** (one of the 5 under that pillar)
3. **Secondary pillar** (only if the article genuinely bridges two stages — e.g., a nail post that ends on a confidence note)
4. **Tags** (3–6, from the master list where possible)
5. **Related articles** (2–3 existing posts to link, same subcategory)
6. **Internal links** (1 cross-pillar "you might also like")
7. **CTA** (matched to pillar per §12)
8. **Placement** (URL slug + where it appears: subcategory page, homepage featured slot, or both)

This is the exact checklist to run every time a new Pin/post gets added — same as the flat-file `post-<slug>.html` system already in place, just with the categorization decision made explicit before the post is generated.

## 7. Main Navigation

```
[Logo]   Explore ▾   Start Here   The Philosophy   The Community   [Search]
```

- **"Explore"** beats "Blog" — it matches the brand's editorial, aspirational tone and implies discovery rather than a chronological feed. Dropdown shows the 3 pillars.
- **"Start Here"** — a dedicated, curated path for new visitors (see §8).
- **"The Philosophy"** — houses the "Beauty is the beginning, Becoming is the mission" brand story (your About page, but framed as philosophy, not bio).
- **"The Community"** — the direct link out to Lady's Leadershift. Keep this visible in nav at all times; it's the destination, not a footer afterthought.

---

## 8. Search + Discovery

- **Search bar** in header (simple keyword search across titles/tags)
- **Filter chips** on the Explore hub: filter by pillar, then by subcategory
- **"Start Here"** — 3 hand-picked posts, one per pillar, ordered to mirror the funnel (Self & Style → Confidence & Identity → Ambitious Life)
- **Featured collections** — seasonal or thematic groupings that cut across subcategories (e.g., "Fall Refresh," "First 30 Days of Confidence") — these are your best tool for surfacing older content without new categories
- **Popular posts** — simple most-viewed list, refreshed monthly
- **Latest posts** — chronological, but never the default homepage view

Keep it to these five. Anything more (elaborate quiz funnels, multi-level filters) overbuilds for where the content library is now and can be layered in later without restructuring anything.

## 12. Internal Linking Strategy

- **Depth links** (2–3 per article): same subcategory, keep the reader in the room she's already in.
- **Progression link** (1 per article): next pillar in the funnel — Self & Style → Confidence & Identity → Ambitious Life. Never skip a stage, never send backward as the featured link.
- **Hub links**: every article links up to its subcategory page and pillar page (breadcrumb + inline mention).
- **Collection links**: seasonal/featured collections pull from multiple subcategories — use these to resurface older posts instead of creating new categories.

---

## 13. Email Capture Strategy

- **Placement:** mid-article (soft, inline) + end-of-article (before the CTA box) + homepage.
- **Offer varies by pillar:**
  - Self & Style → a style/nail-inspiration freebie (low commitment)
  - Confidence & Identity → an identity/worksheet-style lead magnet
  - Ambitious Life → direct invite to the community / Lady's Leadershift waitlist
- **Never gate the article itself** — capture is additive, not a paywall. The Digital Workbook stays the paid product; email capture is the free on-ramp to it.

---

## 14. CTA Strategy (by pillar, matches funnel intensity)

| Pillar | CTA intensity | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Self & Style | Soft | "See what's underneath the look" → Confidence & Identity |
| Confidence & Identity | Medium | Email capture + "Ready for what's next?" → Ambitious Life |
| Ambitious Life | Strong | "Start Your Next Chapter" → Lady's Leadershift |

Every article carries exactly one CTA at this intensity — resist stacking multiple asks on one page.

---

## 15. Rules for Adding Future Categories

- **Don't add a subcategory** until 8–10 existing or planned articles naturally cluster there and none of the current 5-per-pillar subcategories fit.
- **Hard cap:** 7 subcategories per pillar. If you're tempted to add an 8th, it's a sign two existing subcategories should split instead, or the new content belongs in a Featured Collection, not a permanent category.
- **New pillar (4th door):** only if a genuinely new funnel stage emerges that doesn't fit Nails→Self-Image→Confidence→Identity→Community→Success — should be rare, treat as a major brand decision, not a content-ops one.
- **Tag hygiene:** retire/merge tags under 3 uses each quarter (see §5).
- This structure holds at 20, 50, 100, and 500+ articles because growth happens *within* subcategories and Featured Collections, not by adding new top-level doors.

## Build Notes for the Developer

- Keep the existing flat-file pattern (`post-<slug>.html`, shared `style.css`/`logo.png` at repo root) — this architecture layers *on top of* that, it doesn't require restructuring the file system.
- Each post needs 2 new metadata fields beyond what's likely already there: `pillar` and `subcategory` (plus the existing tags array). These three fields alone power the category pages, filter chips, and related-post logic — no new templates needed beyond a pillar/subcategory index page.
- Pillar and subcategory pages can be generated the same way posts are (via the GitHub Actions pipeline) — just two new page types feeding off the same `posts.json`.
