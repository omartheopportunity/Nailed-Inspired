Black Halloween Costumes: Chic, Spooky, and Easy

Sept 30, 2026 · 3 min read

Black halloween costumes are the easiest kind to pull off: you likely own half of it already. Here are the three classics, witch, cat, and vampire, plus a black lace veil look, and how to keep each one modern.

Witch

  • Base: a black dress, jumpsuit, or long skirt and top.
  • Add: a wide-brim hat and a broom or wand.
  • Modern twist: a sheer black cape, silver rings, and a moody plum lip.

Cat

  • Base: black leggings and a fitted top, or a black bodysuit.
  • Add: ears, a tail, and winged liner with small whiskers.
  • Modern twist: a leather jacket and chunky boots.

Vampire

  • Base: a black dress or suit.
  • Add: fangs, a high collar or cape, and a deep red lip.
  • Modern twist: velvet and a satin blouse.

Black lace veil

  • Base: a black slip dress or lace top.
  • Add: a long lace veil over the head or shoulders, gloves, and a dark lip.
  • Modern twist: a mourning-bride or gothic widow look with pearls.

Add texture so black doesn’t look flat

  • Mix fabrics: lace, velvet, satin, leather, and mesh.
  • Add metallic accents in silver or gold.
  • Use one pop of color in your lip, nails, or a single accessory.

Nails for black outfits

A glossy black almond, deep burgundy chrome, or black French tip finishes the look. Short nails work just as well.
