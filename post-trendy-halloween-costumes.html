Trendy Halloween Costumes Everyone Will Be Asking About

Sept 30, 2026 · 3 min read

The best trendy halloween costumes look great in photos, are easy to source, and get the question “where did you get that?” Here are ten that fit, plus a quick way to make any of them photograph well.

10 trendy costumes

  1. Y2K pop star. Rhinestones, butterfly clips, and a low-rise moment.
  2. Barbie-core doll. Bright pink, a box-style prop, and a tight ponytail.
  3. Vampire academic. A dark blazer, plaid skirt, and a book with a fake bite mark.
  4. Alien glam. Iridescent makeup, silver lips, and metallic accessories.
  5. Jelly candy girl. Translucent pastel pieces and glossy jelly nails.
  6. Disco cowgirl. A mirrored hat, fringe, and boots.
  7. Ghostface-inspired glam. A black robe, white makeup, and dramatic lashes.
  8. Retro flight attendant. A scarf, blazer, and a vintage suitcase.
  9. Cottagecore witch. A flowy dress, a basket of “herbs,” and a soft green palette.
  10. Fairy goth. Black tulle wings, dark lips, and glitter under the eyes.

Where to source them

  • Thrift stores and resale apps for blazers, dresses, boots, and vintage pieces.
  • Your own closet for basics like black tees, jeans, and leggings.
  • Craft stores for wings, halos, and glitter.
  • Budget online shops for one accent piece, but order this week.

Make the costume look great in photos

  • Shoot near a window or under warm lighting. Overhead lights are not your friend.
  • Choose one bold color as the anchor.
  • Add one small prop to hold, like a wand, a book, or a phone.
  • Take photos before the party, when your makeup is fresh.
Trends fade by November. The way you carry yourself in the photo doesn’t.
