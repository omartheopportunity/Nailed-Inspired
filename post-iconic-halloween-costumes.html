Iconic Halloween Costumes That Never Go Out of Style

Sept 30, 2026 · 3 min read

Iconic halloween costumes are the ones people recognize instantly, year after year. These classics never go out of style, and each has a simple way to make it feel fresh.

10 classics and how to update them

  1. Vampire. Classic cape and fangs. Freshen it with a jewel-tone lip and velvet.
  2. Witch. Hat, broom, and black dress. Try a deep purple or emerald instead of black.
  3. Ghost. A white sheet. Cut it into a dress or add lace for a modern take.
  4. Pirate. Hat, eyepatch, and boots. Add a corset or a statement belt.
  5. Angel. White and gold with wings. Go dark with black feathers for a fallen angel.
  6. Devil. Red, black, and horns. Try a tailored suit for a sharp twist.
  7. Skeleton. Black with white bones painted on. Try glitter bones or a sugar-skull face.
  8. Cat. Ears and whiskers. Add a leather look or a sequin tail.
  9. Mummy. Gauze wrapped over black. Mix in bandage-style pieces on a dress.
  10. Cowgirl. Hat and boots. Try a rhinestone denim set.

Why classics work

  • People get it right away. No explaining the costume all night.
  • Easy to find. Every store carries the pieces.
  • Easy to personalize. One fresh detail makes it yours.

How to keep it from looking basic

Pick one detail that’s clearly you, whether that’s an unexpected color, a fabric, or a piece of jewelry, and let the rest stay classic.
