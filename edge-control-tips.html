Edge Control Tips

Nov 17, 2026 · 2 min read

Smooth edges look polished and take just minutes. These six tips keep them laid without flaking.

1. Start Clean

Wash and dry edges.

2. Light Amount

A little goes far.

3. Use a Brush

Soft brush strokes.

4. Tie Down

Silk scarf for 10 minutes.

5. Set Gently

Do not overdo it.

6. Moisturize

Do not skip it.
