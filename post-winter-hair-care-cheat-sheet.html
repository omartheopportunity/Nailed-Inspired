Winter Hair Care Cheat Sheet: Soft Hair Routine by Hair Type

Nov 3, 2026 · 5 min read

Cold air, indoor heat and wool scarves strip moisture fast, and the same routine you used in August will not carry you through January. The fix is not more products, it is the right steps for your hair type, on a schedule you can keep. This is the cheat sheet from the pin, in full: straight, wavy, curly and coily, one week at a time.

The 7 pieces of a winter routine

  • Shampoo: a gentle or moisturizing formula, used less often in winter.
  • Conditioner: every wash, left on for a few minutes before rinsing.
  • Hair mask: a weekly deep treatment for moisture and strength.
  • Leave-in: a light cream or spray to lock in hydration after washing.
  • Oil: a few drops on the ends to seal and keep them from splitting.
  • Microfiber towel: blot instead of rubbing to cut frizz and breakage.
  • Satin bonnet or silk pillowcase: protects your hair while you sleep.

Straight hair

  • Wash: every 3 to 4 days with a lightweight shampoo.
  • Mask: every 1 to 2 weeks, mid-lengths to ends only.
  • Finish: a drop of oil on the ends and a silk pillowcase to prevent static and breakage.

Wavy hair

  • Wash: every 2 to 3 days, conditioning every time.
  • Style: a leave-in cream while damp, then scrunch and let it air dry or diffuse.
  • Mask: weekly to keep waves defined instead of puffy.

Curly hair

  • Wash: weekly or every 5 to 7 days, with a moisturizing shampoo.
  • Layer: conditioner, leave-in, then a gel or cream to hold definition.
  • Dry gently: a microfiber towel or t-shirt, never a rough towel.

Coily hair

  • Wash: every 7 to 10 days, with a pre-poo or conditioner first.
  • Moisture then seal: water-based leave-in, then oil or butter to lock it in.
  • Protect: twists, braids or a bonnet at night to keep ends covered.

The week at a glance

Use this as a rhythm, not a rule: wash day anchors the week, a mask goes in once a week, a quick leave-in refresh mid-week, and a bonnet every night. Small and consistent beats complicated and abandoned.

Hair care is the easiest place to practice keeping a promise to yourself, seven nights a week.
