Nail Shapes Chart

Nov 16, 2026 · 2 min read

Not sure what to ask your tech for? This shape chart shows six options side by side so you can walk in with the right name.

1. Almond

Softens the look of the fingers.

2. Coffin

Long and dramatic with a flat tip.

3. Square

Strong and classic.

4. Squoval

Square with soft corners, great for beginners.

5. Stiletto

Sharp and bold, best on a short sidewall.

6. Oval

Gentle and easy to maintain.
