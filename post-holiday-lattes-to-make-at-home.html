7 Holiday Lattes to Make at Home

Nov 3, 2026 · 4 min read

You do not need a coffee shop to make a holiday latte, just espresso or strong coffee, milk, a little syrup and a frother (or a jar you shake). Here are seven holiday lattes to make at home, with the simple build for each. If you saved the pin, this is the full version.

The base (use it for all seven)

  • Coffee: 1 shot of espresso or 1/2 cup of strong brewed coffee.
  • Milk: 3/4 cup, steamed or heated, then frothed. Oat, almond or dairy all work.
  • Sweet: 1 to 2 tablespoons of the syrup or sweetener in each recipe, to taste.

1. Gingerbread latte

Stir in gingerbread syrup (or a spoonful of molasses, brown sugar, ginger and cinnamon). Top with whipped cream and a crumble of gingerbread cookie.

2. Peppermint mocha

Whisk a spoonful of cocoa and a few drops of peppermint extract into the coffee, add sweetener, then milk. Finish with whipped cream and crushed candy cane.

3. Eggnog latte

Swap half the milk for eggnog, heat gently without boiling, and dust with nutmeg. Rich enough that it needs very little sugar.

4. Chestnut praline latte

Use a chestnut praline or toasted-nut syrup, then top with crushed praline or toasted pecans for crunch.

5. Cinnamon dolce latte

Stir brown sugar and cinnamon into the coffee until dissolved. Add milk and a cinnamon sprinkle on top.

6. Sugar cookie latte

Add vanilla and a touch of almond extract plus sweetener. Top with whipped cream and holiday sprinkles.

7. Maple pecan latte

Stir in real maple syrup and a pinch of salt, then top with chopped toasted pecans.

Why it is cheaper

A coffee-shop latte can run several dollars each. Making them at home, with one bag of beans and a few pantry staples, brings each cup way down and lets you control the sweetness. It also turns a drink into a small ritual instead of an errand.
