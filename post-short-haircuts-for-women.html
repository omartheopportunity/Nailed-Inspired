20 Short Haircuts for Women Worth Bringing to Your Stylist

Oct 1, 2026 · 3 min read

Walking into a salon with the right name for what you want saves everyone time. Here are short haircuts for women worth bringing to your stylist, grouped so you can screenshot the ones that fit.

Bobs

  1. Classic bob. One length, chin-skimming.
  2. Angled bob. Longer in front, shorter in back.
  3. Blunt bob. A sharp, even line with no layers.
  4. French bob. Jaw length with a short fringe.
  5. Stacked bob. Shorter layers in the back for lift.

Pixies and crops

  1. Classic pixie. Short sides, longer top.
  2. Long pixie. Extra length on top for styling.
  3. Textured crop. Choppy layers for movement.
  4. Undercut pixie. Shaved or very short sides.
  5. Curly pixie. Cropped layers that follow your curl pattern.

Shags and layers

  1. Short shag. Heavy layers and texture.
  2. Wolf cut. Shaggy layers with volume on top.
  3. Bixie. A blend of bob and pixie.
  4. Choppy bob. A bob with razored ends.
  5. Layered lob. Long and light.

Everyday favorites

  1. Asymmetrical bob. One side longer than the other.
  2. A-line bob. Longer toward the face.
  3. Short bangs and a bob. Fringe that frames the eyes.
  4. Tapered cut. Short at the neck, longer on top.
  5. Soft undercut bob. A hidden shorter layer underneath.

How to ask

  • Say the cut name and show a photo.
  • Mention your texture and how much time you spend styling.
  • Ask what to expect at the six-week mark.
You do not have to guess. Pick a name, bring a photo, and let the stylist do the rest.
