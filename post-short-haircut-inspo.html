Short Haircut Inspo Before You Chop It Off

Oct 1, 2026 · 3 min read

A short haircut is a big decision, and the best way to make it is slowly. Before you book, work through this checklist so the cut you get is the cut you wanted.

Questions to ask yourself first

  1. Why now? A fresh start is a great reason. A bad week is not.
  2. How do I style my hair today? If you air-dry and go, short hair will fit you.
  3. How much time do I have? Some cuts need trims every four to six weeks.

What to look for in inspo photos

  • Photos on hair like yours, in both texture and thickness.
  • Pictures from several angles: front, side, and back.
  • A cut you like on more than one person.

Before the chop

  • Book a consultation and ask what the cut will look like on you.
  • Try on a wig or a photo filter if you are unsure.
  • Consider donating your length if it is long enough. Many programs accept braided ponytails.

The day of

  • Bring photos, including one you do not want.
  • Ask to see the side and back in the mirror before they finish.
  • Take a photo of the result so you can recreate it.

After the chop

  • Give it two weeks to settle.
  • Learn one go-to style and practice it.
Hair grows back. The courage to change does not need to.
