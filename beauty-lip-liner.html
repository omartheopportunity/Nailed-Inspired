How to use it

Three steps. That's it.

  1. Line just outside the darkest edge of your lip, starting at the cupid's bow.
  2. Fill the outer third of the lip with liner so the color has a base to grip.
  3. Top with gloss or lip oil (see the lip combo shelf) and press, don't rub.
