Winter Nail Shades for Dark Skin

Nov 16, 2026 · 2 min read

Winter polish looks different on deep skin. These six shades flatter brown hands in cold weather light, and each is easy to ask for by name.

1. Mocha Mousse

A soft brown that reads expensive.

2. Chocolate Plum

Deep plum with a brown base.

3. Midnight Navy

Navy that looks almost black in low light.

4. Icy Chrome

Silver chrome for a cold weather contrast.

5. Deep Burgundy

A classic that never fails on deep skin.

6. Warm Taupe

A neutral with a hint of rose.
