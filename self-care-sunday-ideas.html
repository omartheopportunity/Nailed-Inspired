Self Care Sunday Ideas

Nov 16, 2026 · 2 min read

Rest is part of the work. Here are eight self care ideas that take a Sunday afternoon and leave you ready for Monday.

1. Bath

Add salts and a candle.

2. Face Mask

Ten quiet minutes.

3. Journal

Write three pages.

4. Walk

No headphones.

5. Call

Someone who gets you.

6. Cook

Make something warm.

7. Read

One chapter.

8. Early Night

Lights out by ten.
