30 Cozy Things to Do This Winter

Nov 3, 2026 · 4 min read

Winter does not have to be a season you wait out. It can be the season you slow down on purpose. Here are 30 cozy things to do this winter, grouped so you can pick one for tonight and one for the weekend. If you saved the pin, this is the checklist.

Tonight, at home (1 to 10)

  • 1. Make a holiday latte or a pot of hot chocolate.
  • 2. Light a candle and turn the overhead lights off.
  • 3. Take a long bath or shower with something that smells like cedar or vanilla.
  • 4. Put on a snowy or holiday movie you love.
  • 5. Read a chapter by candlelight.
  • 6. Wear pajamas all day and call it a plan.
  • 7. Build a blanket fort, no explanation needed.
  • 8. Cook a slow soup or stew.
  • 9. Write in a journal for ten minutes.
  • 10. Do a face mask and a hair mask.

This weekend (11 to 20)

  • 11. Go for a winter walk and notice the quiet.
  • 12. Bake something that makes the whole house smell like it.
  • 13. Visit a local market or holiday pop-up.
  • 14. Play board games or cards with someone you like.
  • 15. Try a new recipe you have been saving.
  • 16. Decorate one corner of your home and make it yours.
  • 17. Declutter one drawer and feel the difference.
  • 18. Make a playlist for the season.
  • 19. Wrap gifts slowly with good paper and a ribbon.
  • 20. Write three handwritten notes to people who matter.

For the woman you are becoming (21 to 30)

  • 21. Write your 90-day plan for the new year.
  • 22. Do a year-in-review: what worked, what did not.
  • 23. List three things you want more of in the new year.
  • 24. Start a morning ritual you can keep: one cup, one page, one goal.
  • 25. Unfollow what makes you feel small.
  • 26. Message one person you admire.
  • 27. Try a nail color or style you have never worn.
  • 28. Learn one new skill for 20 minutes a day.
  • 29. Say no to one thing you do not want.
  • 30. Sit with one question: who am I becoming next?

Start with one

You do not need thirty. Pick one for tonight. Cozy is a decision, and it is one of the easiest ways to treat yourself like someone you are looking after.

Rest is not the opposite of ambition. It is where the next chapter gets written.
