Hairstyles for Short Hair You Can Do in 5 Minutes

Oct 1, 2026 · 3 min read

You do not need a curling iron or a tutorial to make short hair look finished. These five styles take a brush, a clip or two, and about five minutes.

The 5-minute list

  1. Side clip. Part your hair deep, sweep the longer side across, and pin it behind your ear with a gold clip.
  2. Half-up mini bun. Gather the top section, twist it, and secure with a pin or small elastic. Leave the rest down.
  3. Headband push-back. Brush hair back and slide a wide headband on. Pull a few strands forward for softness.
  4. Tucked bob. Tuck one or both sides behind your ears and smooth the crown.
  5. Mini pigtails. Two small low tails near the nape. Messy is fine.

Step by step: the side clip

  1. Brush hair smooth.
  2. Make a deep side part.
  3. Sweep the heavy side across your forehead and behind the ear.
  4. Pin it flat with one or two clips.

Make it last all day

  • Use a pinch of lightweight cream to calm flyaways.
  • Carry two extra pins in your bag.
  • Shake in a little dry shampoo at the roots if your hair falls flat by afternoon.

Finish the look

A clean set of short nails and a bold accessory is a complete outfit on a busy day.

Five minutes is enough when you stop trying to fix everything and start choosing one detail.
