Short Hair Styles That Look Good on Everyone

Oct 1, 2026 · 3 min read

Short hair is not one look. It is a dozen, and the one that works for you depends on your texture, your face shape, and how much time you want to spend. Here is how to match the cut to you.

Six short styles to know

  1. Bob. Chin length, blunt or soft. The most flexible short cut and a good first step.
  2. Pixie. Cropped close at the sides and back with length on top. Bold and low maintenance.
  3. Shag. Layered and textured. It adds movement to fine, straight hair.
  4. Lob. A long bob that grazes the collarbone. It is the safest cut if you are nervous.
  5. Curly crop. Short layers cut dry to shape your curls and control volume.
  6. Bixie. A bob and pixie hybrid with soft layers and a fringe.

Match the cut to your texture

  • Fine or straight: blunt bobs and shags add the look of thickness.
  • Wavy: layered bobs and bixies let waves sit naturally.
  • Curly or coily: ask for a stylist who cuts curls dry, and keep length where your curls spring up.

Match the cut to your face shape

  • Round: a bob just past the chin, with side-swept length, adds definition.
  • Oval: almost any cut works, so choose by lifestyle.
  • Square: soft layers and waves soften a strong jawline.
  • Heart: chin-length bobs and side-swept fringe balance a wider forehead.

Before you book

  • Bring three photos and one you do not want.
  • Ask how often it needs a trim to hold its shape.
  • Ask how it will look at three months, not just on day one.
A good short cut is not about losing length. It is about choosing the shape that makes the rest of you easier to see.
