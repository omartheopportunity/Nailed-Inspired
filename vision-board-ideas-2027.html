2027 Vision Board Categories

Nov 16, 2026 · 2 min read

A vision board works best with categories. Use these eight to build a 2027 board that covers your whole life.

1. Money

A number you want to reach.

2. Business

The thing you will launch.

3. Health

How you want to feel.

4. Home

The space you are building.

5. Travel

One place you will go.

6. Community

The people in your room.

7. Style

How you will show up.

8. Peace

What you will let go.
