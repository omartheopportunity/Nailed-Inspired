Before you order

What sizes does the wrap come in?

Each pattern is offered as a 30″ × 36″ roll or a longer 30″ × 72″ roll, and ships rolled.

What is the paper like?

Matte, one-sided, 90gsm paper with a seamless repeating pattern.

Is it made to order?

Yes, each roll is made to order. Current shipping timing and prices are always shown on the Etsy listing, so check there before you order for a specific date.

Where do I check out?

Wrap checkout happens on Etsy. The coffee is on The Daily Roast page.
