Halloween Costume Inspo: 20 Ideas Worth Saving

Sept 30, 2026 · 3 min read

Here is halloween costume inspo for solo looks, duos, and groups: 20 ideas in total. Scroll, save, and pick your favorite before October runs out.

Solo looks (8)

  1. Black cat
  2. Fortune teller with a scarf, coin jewelry, and a crystal ball
  3. Mermaid with shell top and sequin skirt
  4. Roaring Twenties flapper
  5. Vampire with a velvet cape
  6. Mad scientist with a lab coat and goggles
  7. Fairy with wings and glitter
  8. Classic witch with a pointed hat

Duo costumes (6)

  1. Salt and pepper
  2. Sun and moon
  3. Peanut butter and jelly
  4. Pirate and mermaid
  5. Angel and devil
  6. Cowboy and cowgirl

Group costumes (6)

  1. Crayon box, one color each
  2. Deck of cards
  3. Flock of flamingos in pink tutus
  4. Bag of candy, each person a different treat
  5. Zodiac signs, each friend dressed as their own
  6. Fruit salad

Group tip: treat bags

If your group goes with the candy idea, hand out small wrapped treats at the party. Halloween wrapping paper makes even a $2 gift look intentional.

How to choose

  • Solo: pick the look you can move in. You’ll wear it all night.
  • Duo: pick a concept that works even if only one of you can attend.
  • Group: choose costumes that don’t depend on everyone having the same budget.

Save this list, then send it to whoever you’re going with.
