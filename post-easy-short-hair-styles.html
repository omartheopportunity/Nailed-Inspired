Easy Short Hair Styles for Lazy Mornings

Oct 1, 2026 · 3 min read

Some mornings you have ten minutes and no plan. These easy short hair styles look put together without asking much of you.

The lazy-morning list

  1. Air-dry texture. Scrunch in a light cream and leave it.
  2. Side-swept and clipped. One clip and you are done.
  3. Quick wave. Run a flat iron through each section once.
  4. Slicked back. A little gel, a comb, and you are out the door.
  5. Headband and go. Brush back, add the headband, tousle the front.

A 10-minute routine

  1. Shake the hair out and brush it into place.
  2. Add cream to damp hair or dry shampoo to day-two hair.
  3. Pick one detail: a clip, a part, or a headband.

Night-before tips

  • Sleep on a silk pillowcase or wrap.
  • Loosely twist damp hair for easy waves.
  • Lay out the clip and headband.

Done is better than perfect

Pair the style with one intentional accessory and call it finished.

Looking put together is mostly deciding you are finished.
