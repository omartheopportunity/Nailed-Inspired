15 Halloween Costumes for College Girls You Can Pull Together in a Day

Sept 30, 2026 · 3 min read

You have a party tonight, a budget that says ramen, and a closet that says “maybe.” These halloween costumes college students actually wear take one trip to a store or, better, no trip at all. Every idea below is cheap, easy, and party-ready by dinner.

Closet raids (under $10)

  1. Black cat. Black leggings, black top, cat-ear headband, eyeliner whiskers.
  2. Witch. A black dress plus a witch hat is the whole costume. Add purple lipstick.
  3. Cowgirl. Jeans, boots, a plaid shirt tied at the waist, and a hat.
  4. Angel. White dress or tee, a halo headband, and glitter on your shoulders.
  5. Devil. Red top, black bottoms, horn headband, red lip.
  6. Vampire. All black, red lip, and drugstore fangs.

Cheap add-ons (under $20)

  1. Pirate. Striped shirt, belt, bandana, and a hat.
  2. Bee. Yellow top, black tape stripes, wings from a craft store.
  3. Ghost. A white sheet with cut eyes, or a white dress with gray face paint.
  4. Mermaid. Green or teal sequins, shell top, and iridescent eyeshadow.
  5. Flapper. Fringe dress, pearls, and a headband.
  6. Sailor. Navy and white stripes, a neckerchief, and red lipstick.

Group-friendly ideas

  1. Crayons. Everyone in a different color, with a paper cone hat.
  2. Deck of cards. Each friend is a card, with cardboard signs.
  3. Fruit salad. Grab colorful tees and print a fruit sticker for each.

Bringing a gift to the party?

Host gifts and friend-group swaps are part of the season. Wrap yours in Halloween wrapping paper so it looks as put together as your costume.

Make any costume better

  • Finish with the nails. A themed set of short gel-polish nails ties the whole look together.
  • Test your face makeup before the party, not in the bathroom at 9 p.m.
  • Choose shoes you can stand in for four hours. Your feet do not care about your costume.
The costume is the excuse. Showing up as yourself, on purpose, is the point.
