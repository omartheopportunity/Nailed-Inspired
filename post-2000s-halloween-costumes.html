2000s Halloween Costumes: Y2K Looks Worth Bringing Back

Sept 30, 2026 · 3 min read

A 2000s halloween costume is peak nostalgia: low-rise jeans, rhinestones, and flip phones. These Y2K looks are worth bringing back, and each comes with the pieces that sell it.

8 looks

  1. Early-2000s pop princess. Low-rise jeans, a baby tee, butterfly clips, and glossy lips.
  2. Flip phone girl. Any Y2K outfit, plus a flip phone and a matching charm strap.
  3. Juicy tracksuit. A velour set, a rhinestone belt, and chunky highlights.
  4. Bratz-style doll. Glam makeup, a fur-trim jacket, a mini skirt, and platform boots.
  5. Emo scene kid. Striped arm warmers, skinny jeans, a studded belt, and side-swept bangs.
  6. Trucker hat and tube top. A trucker hat, tube top, cargo pants, and a mini bag.
  7. Mean Girls-style plastics. Pink, a tank top, a mini skirt, and a cut-out shirt for fun.
  8. MySpace profile picture. Selfie pose, heavy eyeliner, and a caption sign.

Shopping list

  • Accessories: butterfly clips, claw clips, rhinestone belts, and a mini shoulder bag.
  • Nails: chrome, glitter, or a short French with a rhinestone.
  • Makeup: glossy lips, thin brows, frosty eyeshadow, and body glitter.
  • Hair: crimped, half-up clips, or side-swept bangs.

Where to find real pieces

Thrift stores and resale apps carry actual early-2000s clothes. Check your own family’s closet, too.

Nostalgia tip

Play the era’s playlist while you get ready. It helps the character come through in your walk and your poses.
