6 Things to Leave Behind Before 2027

Sept 2, 2026 · 6 min read

A new chapter isn't always about adding more.

Sometimes it's about carrying less.

Old beliefs. Old habits. Old relationships. Old expectations. Old versions of yourself.

If you want to enter 2027 differently, consider what needs to stay in the year you're leaving behind.

1. The Need for Everyone's Approval

You cannot build a meaningful life while requiring everyone to understand your decisions.

Someone may not understand your career. Your goals. Your boundaries. Your ambition. Your lifestyle.

That's okay.

Approval feels safe, but it can become expensive when you trade your own direction for it.

You don't need everyone's permission to grow.

2. Constant Comparison

Someone will always be ahead of you in one area.

Someone will always have more followers. More money. A better body. A bigger house. A more impressive career.

Comparison turns someone else's highlight reel into evidence against your own life.

Instead, measure your progress against who you were.

That's a comparison you can actually use.

3. Relationships That Require You to Shrink

You don't need to hate someone to realize the relationship isn't healthy for your growth.

If you constantly feel like you have to minimize yourself, hide your ambition, tolerate disrespect, or become smaller to maintain the relationship, pay attention.

Some relationships need boundaries. Some need distance. Some simply need a new level of access.

4. The Identity You Outgrew

Stop introducing yourself to yourself through your past.

Yes, you've made mistakes. Yes, you've struggled. Yes, certain things didn't work.

But those experiences don't have to become permanent personality traits.

You are allowed to update your self-concept.

5. Waiting Until You Feel Ready

Confidence often comes after action, not before it.

If you wait until you're completely prepared, you may wait indefinitely.

Take the first step. Learn. Adjust. Improve. Then take the next one.

Readiness is often built through movement.

6. The Belief That You Are Behind

Your life isn't late.

You're not required to follow someone else's timeline.

Maybe your path has taken longer. Maybe you've changed directions. Maybe you're starting over. Maybe you're rebuilding.

None of that makes your future smaller.

Stop using someone else's timeline as evidence against your own potential.

Make Room for What's Next

Leaving things behind isn't always dramatic.

Sometimes it looks like: deleting the number, changing the conversation, saying no, unfollowing the account, joining a new community, applying for the opportunity, forgiving yourself, trying again.

The goal isn't to become someone with no past.

It's to stop letting your past control your future.

Some things were lessons — not luggage.
