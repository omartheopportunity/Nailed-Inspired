Questions

Before you order

How do I use them?

Body Wash: wet your body, pour a small amount into your hands, lather gently in circular motions and rinse. Conditioner: after shampooing, apply a small amount through your hair, leave it on for a couple of minutes, then rinse.

How big are they?

Each tube is 200 ml.

How long do they last on the shelf?

Best before 36 months from the manufacturing date printed on the tube.

Who makes them?

RZN Beauty products are manufactured in Egypt by the Egyptian Company for Cosmetics exclusively for Risen Live, and carry the Approved Risen Live Product mark.

Any precautions?

For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes and rinse with water if irritation occurs. Don't use on irritated skin, and consult a professional if you have sensitivity or skin concerns. Store in a cool place away from direct sunlight and keep out of reach of children.

What is Nailed & Inspired's connection to these products?

We're a Risen Live customer and love what we use, so we share it with our community. Orders are placed and fulfilled through Risen Live.
