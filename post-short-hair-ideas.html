Short Hair Ideas to Save for Your Next Cut

Oct 1, 2026 · 3 min read

Save this list for your next appointment. These short hair ideas span lengths, colors, and textures so you can pick a direction and bring it to your stylist.

By length

  • Ear-length pixie. Short and easy.
  • Chin bob. Classic and flexible.
  • Collarbone lob. Short enough to feel new.

By color

  • Soft honey blonde. Warm and bright.
  • Deep espresso. Rich and glossy.
  • Copper. A bold, confident tone.
  • Silver. Striking at any age.

By texture

  • Sleek and straight. Polished and modern.
  • Beachy waves. Relaxed and easy.
  • Defined curls. Shape and spring.

Details to add

  • Curtain bangs.
  • A subtle undercut.
  • Face-framing pieces.

Build your board

  1. Save five photos you love.
  2. Note what they have in common.
  3. Show your stylist the pattern, not just the pictures.
A good idea becomes a good cut once someone else can see it too.
